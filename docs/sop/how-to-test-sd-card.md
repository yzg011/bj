How to Test Your SD Card?

The information here is compiled from online sources. Recording stability is often affected by SD card quality. This guide helps you check your SD card's status. All tools and tutorials mentioned come from the internet. For further assistance, please contact the SD card manufacturer.

---

Recommended Tool: H2testw

This free tool performs a full read/write verification to check your SD card's health.

How to Use

1. Prepare your SD card: Insert it into your computer. Back up any important data first, then format the card.
    
    ---
    
2. Download & run: Get H2testw from the link below and launch it.
    
    [https://h2testw.org/](https://h2testw.org/)
    
    ---
    
3. Select your card:
    
    Click Select target, browse to find your SD card, and select it < Click Refresh to double-check the correct drive is chosen.
    
    ![](https://alidocs.dingtalk.com/core/api/resources/img/5eecdaf48460cde5e22c70dd5ae86f84ddd297ed1aa94b9575b8339e1c4c2483a6e113e55758f0bd39e8703ac5556d0d099d44f7dad03dfb56ee4a30c39cb379f33197f6af7decf8602b76f5459f3c189592bf6f409b3274093d117f5a9a2837?tmpCode=6cb9a156-42a4-403c-8fa5-4b4abbc81b79) ![](https://alidocs.dingtalk.com/core/api/resources/img/5eecdaf48460cde5e22c70dd5ae86f84ddd297ed1aa94b9575b8339e1c4c2483a6e113e55758f0bd39e8703ac5556d0d249c7998280a62501ae13379e9823bea076e8d1bbfc3ec415984ee6c4a8c0dcb6711a9233209978a1a88d3f8098878e8?tmpCode=6cb9a156-42a4-403c-8fa5-4b4abbc81b79)
    
    ---
    
4. Start the test: Click Write + Verify. The tool will write data to the card segment by segment and then read it back for verification.
    
    Important: This test can take a long time, especially for large-capacity cards. Please do not remove the SD card or close the software during the process.
    
    ![](https://alidocs.dingtalk.com/core/api/resources/img/5eecdaf48460cde5e22c70dd5ae86f84ddd297ed1aa94b9575b8339e1c4c2483a6e113e55758f0bd39e8703ac5556d0ddf368fbc39e9272c1fa13a4010d6fb81d09f021be5bfc77808849826366eea79bfab61e70a93cfc24bafd8ac4d231619?tmpCode=6cb9a156-42a4-403c-8fa5-4b4abbc81b79)
    
    ---
    
5. Review the results:
    
    - If errors appear: The SD card may be faulty. Please replace it or contact the card's manufacturer for support.
        
    - If the test without errors: Your SD card is in good working order. The software window will show the test data.
        
6. Final step: After the test is complete, format the SD card again before using it normally.
